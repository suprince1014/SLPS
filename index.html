01 A little about us

Honouring our heritage.
Empowering our people.

Saurashtra Leuva Patel Samaj (SLPS) Vapi is a community organization and social institution established in 2007. We bring people together to preserve Indian culture and heritage, support education and philanthropy, and stand alongside members of our community who need a helping hand.
